The Problem Statement

  • Rapid EV Adoption: Electric vehicle adoption is accelerating rapidly, creating fluctuating and unmanaged grid loads.
  • Capacity Imbalances: Some public stations experience severe peak congestion while others remain chronically underutilized.
  • Silent Station Faults: Unresolved station disruptions (offline chargers, GFCI trips) leave drivers stranded and damage operator trust.
  • Lack of Real-Time Forecasting: Operators lack granular next-hour load forecasts to procure clean energy and plan storage dispatch.

Future Scope & Extensions (Slide 11)

  • Weather & Grid Telemetry: Integrate real-time ambient temperature and solar irradiation API streams to refine seasonal forecasting.
  • Hardware Fault Sensors: Augment proxy 'Ended By' indicators with direct IoT hardware sensor telemetry and OCPP error messages.
  • Automated Retraining: Continuous MLOps pipeline on cloud platforms (AWS / GCP) with automated model registry versioning.
  • Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G): Bidirectional dispatch recommendations based on predicted regional grid peak hours.
